DESCRIPTION
This release provides the reproducibility package associated with the revised manuscript “Advanced Drone Sensing Framework for Smart X Situational Intelligence.” It contains the Streamlit prototype, exact controlled synthetic pollutant-weather, telemetry, and image-feature inputs, two-branch Isolation Forest and transparent rule-based scoring code, fixed experimental configuration, and paper-supporting outputs for baseline anomaly detection, alpha sensitivity, modality ablation, and controlled-noise robustness. The controlled mission contains 160 records (114 normal and 46 anomalous: 18 pollution-plume, 16 fire-smoke, and 12 industrial-dust records). Both branches use 200-tree Isolation Forest models with contamination 0.10 and fixed seeds 12 and 24. The baseline ensemble uses alpha=0.60 and operational thresholds 0.45/0.60/0.75. The archive also includes persistent human-validation support, a standalone experiment runner, locked dependencies, a data dictionary, and manuscript-to-artifact mapping. All data and sample images are synthetic. Reported quantitative results assess agreement with controlled synthetic labels and demonstrate reproducible framework feasibility; they are not field-validated accuracy or deployment-performance claims. Digital Twin, Data Lakehouse, and distributed edge-cloud services remain architectural-readiness components and are not implemented as production services in this release.
